# Research substrate DOX

## Purpose

- Own durable Mycelium research and trace documentation for repository investigations.

## Local Contracts

- Keep trace documents evidence-based, repository-specific, and suitable for later planning or review.
- Treat traces as historical analysis, not runtime configuration, executable policy, or proof that an unperformed validation succeeded.
- Do not store credentials, tokens, private conversation data, raw provider transcripts, or other secrets.
- Distinguish source-verified behavior, inference, design limitations, and findings that still require reproduction.
